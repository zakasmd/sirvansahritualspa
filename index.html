ABOUT OUR SANCTUARY

The Art of Deep Relaxation & Healing

"Shirvanshah Ritual Massage Baku" is a high-end wellness retreat welcoming international and local guests into an authentic oasis of hygiene, tranquility, and therapeutic excellence.

Nestled inside the prestigious Shirvanshah complex, our facilities feature an authentic heated marble hammam, aromatic Finnish sauna, and bespoke VIP massage suites. Every ritual is conducted with personalized care to ensure you leave completely refreshed and revitalized.

Private VIP Chambers

Completely sterile, soundproof, and private spaces tailored for your peace of mind.

Authentic Bath & Sauna

Natural stone kurnas, exfoliating silk mitten (kese), dense lather, and cedarwood dry sauna.

Night Service Until 03:00

Open daily until 3:00 AM, accommodating your schedule after a busy business day or flight.
